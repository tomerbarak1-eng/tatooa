# Group 12 - Tattoo App Specification - TATOOA

Tomer Barak, Shoham Atias, and Shiri Shalal

## 1. Executive Summary

The app is a Two-Sided Marketplace connecting tattoo enthusiasts with tattoo artists using AI-based simulation and depth-mapping technology. The platform bridges the visual and budgetary expectation gaps for clients, streamlines the Lead Qualification process for artists, and provides a secure, monitored environment for transactions. The goal is to make the inspiration and planning stage tangible and to convert users into paying clients at the studio.

## 2. Business Model

The operational model is based on a value network, avoiding entry barriers for end-users to generate a Network Effect:

- **B2C (End Users):** Freemium. Basic simulation and a general price estimation are provided for free to drive traffic and build a database (Data Acquisition).
- **B2B (Tattoo Artists):** The MVP business model will be based on a Deposit-based Commission. Joining the platform will be free for artists. Revenue will be generated by charging a 15% commission (which will serve as the appointment deposit) at the time of booking via the app, and the artist will collect the remaining balance (85%) at the studio. This model removes entry barriers for artists and ensures client attendance. The artist pays a fixed commission for every client who books a consultation or an appointment through the app. Alternatively, artists can pay a subscription fee to access tools for calendar management, automated lead filtering, sponsored promotion, and a deposit clearing system to prevent cancellations.
- **Disintermediation Prevention:** To prevent users from moving to Instagram to close the deal, we will create a Lock-in mechanism: The client will receive 'Tattoo Insurance' for free touch-ups only if they book through the platform. For the artist, the AI sketches will feature a Watermark that will only be removed and converted into a clean working stencil once the deposit is cleared in the system.

## 3. Target Audiences

- **Demand Side (Potential Clients):** Individuals preparing for their first or an additional tattoo who struggle to visualize how the sketch will sit on their body and are concerned about price gaps. This includes a dedicated audience seeking Cover-Up solutions for old tattoos or scar concealment.
- **Supply Side (Artists and Studios):** Licensed artists who spend valuable time on "ping-pong" communication with unready clients (sending sketches, answering questions about size and pricing). The platform provides them with clients who have a ready-made "project portfolio."

## 4. Product Scope & MVP

- **Must Have (MVP Requirements):** The ability to upload a sketch and simulate it on a standard (2D) image, receive a basic price estimation, and send a request for a quote (lead) to tattoo artists.
- **Nice to Have (Advanced Stages):** LiDAR/Video mapping, skin tone analysis, and a future "Fading Calculator" mechanism.
- **Out of Scope (For MVP):** A full CRM system for artist calendar management, in-app deposit clearing (will be done externally in the first stage), and an automated license verification process.
- **Tech Feasibility:** The AR simulation in the MVP will be based on existing augmented reality libraries (ARKit/ARCore) to detect body surfaces using a standard RGB camera. Depth sensors (LiDAR) will be used as an advanced feature to improve proportion accuracy on premium devices only. The Fallback solution for older devices will be uploading a static 2D image of the body part, onto which the client will manually position the sketch.

## 5. User Flow

### 5.1. User Stories

- **First-time client:** "As a user who is anxious about getting a first tattoo, I want to upload a picture of my forearm and see how a flower sketch will look on it, so I can feel confident that the size and placement are right for me before booking an appointment."
- **Cover-Up client:** "As a user with an old tattoo I dislike, I want to overlay new sketches onto a picture of my existing tattoo, so I can know if a certain style will successfully cover it completely without looking like a black blob."
- **Artist receiving a lead:** "As a busy tattoo artist, I want to receive an inquiry from a client that already includes the tattoo placement, requested size, and budget, so I can save time on basic questions and filter out clients who do not fit my pricing."

### 5.2. B2C User Journey

- **Style & Inspiration:** The user enters a text prompt, uploads reference images, or scans an existing tattoo for a Cover-Up. They select a style (e.g., Realism, Blackwork, Minimalism).
- **Depth-Mapping & Privacy:** The user takes a 3-second video of the tattoo area. The system maps the body's curves, automatically blurring intimate areas and encrypting sensitive data.
- **AR / AI Generation:** The system overlays the sketch onto the image/video while accounting for anatomical distortion. The algorithm automatically adjusts ink saturation to the user's skin tone and includes a "Fading Simulation" option showing how the tattoo will look in 5 years.
- **Social Try-on:** Integration of a quick-share mechanism to Stories/WhatsApp, allowing the user to get feedback from their peers before making a decision (serves as a viral growth engine).
- **Dynamic Pricing Engine:** The user inputs a maximum budget. The algorithm returns a reasonable price range based on complexity, size, and placement.
- **Matchmaking:** Displaying 3-5 local tattoo artists with matching portfolios and price quotes. The client sends a booking request and authorizes a deposit charge if an appointment is confirmed.

### 5.3. B2B User Journey (Artist)

- **Digital Artist Profile & Verification:** The artist completes an Onboarding process that includes verifying their business license/certificate to ensure a standard of quality and sterility. The studio page displays their portfolio, specialization, location, and ratings.
- **Lead Intake System (Dedicated CRM):** The artist receives a "client portfolio" containing the simulation, size, style, skin tone mapping, and budget.
- **End-to-End Client Management:** The artist can approve the appointment/price or propose an alternative. The system provides infrastructure for sending digital health declaration forms prior to arrival and Aftercare instructions post-tattoo.

## 6. Core Technology & Algorithms

- **AR / Depth Models Simulation:** Utilizing computer vision APIs to detect skin topography. Integrating a Fallback mechanism for non-LiDAR devices, enabling processing based on a standard camera.
- **Multivariate Pricing Engine:** A Machine Learning (ML) updated database of tattoo prices, featuring anomaly detection to alert users of unrealistic budgets.
- **MVP Pilot Stage:** Pricing data will initially rely on manual collection of price lists and a market survey of about 50 artists. In advanced stages, the system will transition to an updating ML database that identifies pricing trends automatically.
- **Melanin & Cover-up Mapping:** A visual engine that analyzes skin brightness (or an existing tattoo/scar) and adjusts ink Opacity to present a realistic, illusion-free preview, especially for lighter colors.

## 7. Business Challenges & Solutions (Risk Management)

- **Cold Start Problem:** Offering a free trial period for top tattoo artists in exchange for uploading their portfolios, and using the simulation generator as an independent viral tool on social media (with a Watermark).
- **Platform Leakage:** Integrating an in-app Deposit payment mechanism. Artists will prefer closing appointments through the platform to guarantee attendance (preventing No-Shows), justifying the commission they absorb. The client does not pay extra for the tattoo, giving them no financial incentive to bypass the app.
- **Intellectual Property (IP):** Adding strict terms of use stating that AI-generated sketches based on references are for inspiration only, protecting the system and artists from copyright claims by other creators.
- **Regulation & Medical Liability:** Incorporating a formal legal disclaimer clarifying that the simulation is not a medical commitment, requires legal age verification, and does not replace medical advice regarding ink allergies or existing scars.
- **Technological Risk (Simulation Reliability):** The difficulty of mapping a flat sketch onto a curved body and complex anatomy without looking artificial. Solution: Creating an early technological Proof of Concept (PoC) to test topological distortion on various body parts, and gathering feedback from tattoo artists on the realism level before fully developing the algorithm.

## 8. Recommended Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Try-On Conversion Rate:** The percentage of users who completed the simulation process out of total downloads. Pilot target: 40%.
- **Lead Conversion Rate:** The percentage of simulations that resulted in sending a quote request to a tattoo artist. Pilot target: 15%.
- **Booking Rate:** The percentage of leads that concluded with a cleared deposit and a scheduled appointment on the calendar. Pilot target: 5%.
- **Social Virality (K-Factor):** The number of new users acquired as a result of sharing simulations on social media (Social Try-On). Pilot target: 1.1.
